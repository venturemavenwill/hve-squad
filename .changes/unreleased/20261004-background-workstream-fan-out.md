- **Background workstream fan-out ("live delivery").** In interactive mode, independent workstreams with disjoint write sets (`delivery=background` never overrides disjointness) run in background while the coordinator keeps talking to the user. Coordinator-only gates (discovery, intake, council, Cost Preflight, Risk Gate, routing tiers) run in the foreground first; only a Bounded Lane workstream or one with a confirmed plan launches in background, and an unplanned one gets its Research and Plan first and a second approval before implementation. A bounded workstream is dispatched by the coordinator directly as background `task`s, its owners on the bounded pick and then a reviewer the coordinator resolved from the roster, distinct from every owner, so owners keep their own tools. Only multi-stage workstreams use the new non-user-invocable `Squad Workstream Lead` (`squad-src/.github/agents/squad/squad-workstream-lead.agent.md`), pinned to Claude Sonnet 5 with no `tools:` list; its turns are recorded through `orchestration.leadConsumption`. Launches stay within `COPILOT_SUBAGENT_MAX_CONCURRENT` (else 4). The coordinator verifies artifacts and reads the review verdict from disk, treats a failed dispatch as not delivered, and writes each hand-off one at a time. `Write-SquadHandoff.ps1` accepts an optional `workstream` id (recorded in every entry); with it, `launchedAt` and `since` are required, a review-class record is required, and a deliverable already credited and unmodified since is refused. It also appends a missing Cost Comparison section, and a missing Cost Preflight object with no ceiling needs no write. The coordinator gains the read-only `read_agent` tool, and `squad.prompt.md` gains `[delivery=background]`. In one live run before the direct-dispatch change, a client question was answered at 286 s while three workstreams ran, each passing its review.
